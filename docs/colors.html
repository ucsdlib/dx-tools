Design system

Colors

Our internal tools are inspired by the campus brand colors, adapted to make it clear these are internal tools. Accent colors become primaries; brand primaries provide structural depth. All primary text meets WCAG 2.1 AAA (7:1).

Color strategy

UC San Diego brand guidelines designate Navy and Blue as primary colors, with Turquoise and Citron as supporting accents. For a tool interface, this is deliberately inverted:

Context Background Primary CTA Secondary Body text
Frame (dark) Navy #182B49 Turquoise Citron White — 16:1 ✅
Frame (light) Sand UC San Diego Blue Gold Navy — 14.5:1 ✅
Preview area White UC San Diego Blue Yellow Navy — 14.5:1 ✅

Contrast tables

All key combinations verified. WCAG AAA normal text requires 7:1; large text (18pt+ or 14pt+ bold) requires 4.5:1. Non-text UI elements require 3:1.
